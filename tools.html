Quick Actions
💬
Clear Cache & Reset Microsoft Teams
Stops Teams, wipes cache (keeps Backgrounds), resets the app package, then relaunches fresh. Fixes login loops, crashes & display glitches.
🔄
Run GPUpdate
Forces an immediate refresh of your Group Policy settings from the Barclays domain. No reboot needed.
📅
Outlook – Teams Meeting Add-in
Fixes the missing "New Teams Meeting" button in Outlook. Stops both apps, clears the Teams cache, resets the Teams package, then restarts Outlook & Teams.
⏱️
System Uptime
Shows how long your PC has been running. If uptime exceeds 1 day, a restart is recommended to improve performance and apply pending updates.
