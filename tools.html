IT Utility Tools

Click a button — the action runs instantly on your machine.

⚠️ First time? This tool only works after the IT Tools agent has been installed on your PC by your IT team via SCCM. If buttons do nothing, contact the helpdesk.
When you click a button, Windows will ask "Allow this page to open IT Tools?" — click Open to proceed. A PowerShell window will appear and show progress.

⚠️ Restart your PC?

Your PC will restart in 60 seconds after you click Confirm. Please save all open files right now.

If you change your mind, open a Command Prompt and type   shutdown /a  to cancel.
